Step 1
Create Your Account

Register for a free account using your email address. Our secure Django authentication system encrypts your credentials and protects your data from the moment you sign up. The process takes under two minutes — no credit card required.

📧 Email Registration🔒 Secure Passwords⚡ Under 2 Minutes
Step 2
Create or Join a Family

Start fresh by creating a new family unit and becoming its administrator, or accept an invitation to join an existing tree. Either way, you're instantly connected to a shared genealogical workspace.

🏠 New Family✉️ Join via Invite👑 Admin Controls

Step 3
Add Family Members

Populate your tree by adding profiles for each family member. Record essential details — full name, date of birth, gender, nationality, biographical notes, and profile photos. Each person becomes a richly documented node in your family's history.

👤 Rich Profiles📅 Life Events📸 Photos
Step 4
Define Relationships

Connect family members by defining relationships — parent, child, spouse, or sibling. The system validates every connection in real time, preventing contradictions and ensuring genealogical accuracy.

Explore Your Family Tree

Open the interactive D3.js visualization and explore your family's full structure. Navigate through generations, expand and collapse branches, zoom into family clusters, and click any node to view a full profile.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Do I need to pay to start?

No. Creating an account and building your family tree is completely free. Core genealogy tools are always available at no cost.

Can multiple family members collaborate?

Absolutely. Use the invitation system to bring in relatives worldwide. Set permission levels — viewer, editor, or administrator.

Is my family data private?

Yes. Every family tree is private by default. Only members you invite can see your data. We never sell or share your information.

Can I import existing genealogy data?

We support GEDCOM imports, the industry-standard format. Upload your existing research and we'll map it into your new family tree.
