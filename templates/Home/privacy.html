Privacy Policy

We believe your family's history belongs to you — and only you. Here is exactly how we protect it.

📋

Last Updated: January 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the Family Tree Web Application collects, uses, and protects your information. By using our platform, you agree to these terms.

Our Core Privacy Promise

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers. Your family's information is used solely to provide and improve the Family Tree Web Application.

🔑1. Data Ownership

You own your data. Every piece of information you add — names, dates, photos, biographical notes, and relationship records — belongs entirely to you and your family.

  • You retain full intellectual property rights over all content you create.
  • You can export your complete family tree at any time in PDF or GEDCOM format.
  • You can permanently delete your account and all associated data with a single request.
  • We do not claim any ownership or license over your family's genealogical records.
  • Upon account deletion, all your data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

🌳2. Private Family Trees

Privacy is the default — not an option you turn on. Every family tree is private from the moment of creation and accessible only to people you explicitly invite.

  • All new trees are set to "Private" by default — no one can view them without your approval.
  • You control the exact list of people who can access each tree you own.
  • Invited collaborators can be assigned read-only or edit permissions independently.
  • You can revoke any collaborator's access at any time, immediately.
  • Tree contents are never indexed by search engines or publicly discoverable.
  • Living persons' sensitive details are automatically obscured from non-admin viewers.

🔐3. Secure Authentication

Your account security is built on Django's hardened authentication system — protecting millions of applications worldwide.

  • Passwords are hashed using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 — never stored in plain text.
  • All web forms are protected with CSRF (Cross-Site Request Forgery) tokens.
  • Session cookies use HttpOnly and Secure flags to prevent client-side access.
  • Login attempts are rate-limited to prevent brute-force attacks.
  • Password reset links are single-use and expire within 24 hours.
  • All connections use TLS/HTTPS encryption in transit.

👥4. Access Control

Our multi-layer permission system ensures the right people see the right information — and nothing more. Access is explicit, audited, and revocable.

  • Owner: Full control — view, edit, invite, and delete the tree.
  • Editor: Can add and edit members, but cannot manage permissions.
  • Viewer: Read-only access — cannot make any changes.
  • Our internal staff cannot access your tree contents without an explicit support request from you.
  • All access events are logged with timestamps and IP addresses for your review.

🛡️5. Data Protection

We implement technical and organisational safeguards to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or destruction at every infrastructure layer.

  • All data is stored in encrypted PostgreSQL databases on secured servers.
  • Database backups are performed daily and retained for 30 days, encrypted at rest.
  • Our servers are hosted in ISO 27001-certified data centers with physical security controls.
  • We regularly perform security audits and vulnerability assessments.
  • Affected users will be notified within 72 hours in the event of a confirmed breach.

📊6. What Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our service. We do not collect, sell, or use your data for advertising.

  • Account Data: Name, email address, and hashed password upon registration.
  • Family Tree Data: All genealogical records you choose to add — stored securely and owned by you.
  • Usage Logs: Activity logs within your tree for your benefit.
  • Technical Logs: Server access logs retained for 90 days for security purposes only.
  • Cookies: Session cookies for authentication — no tracking or advertising cookies.

⚖️7. Responsible Data Usage

The data you provide is used exclusively to operate and improve the Family Tree Web Application.

  • ✅ We use your data to display your family tree and provide platform features.
  • ✅ We use your email for account security notifications and password resets.
  • ✅ We use anonymized, aggregated usage data to improve platform performance.
  • ❌ We never sell your personal data to any third party.
  • ❌ We never share your data with advertisers or marketing companies.
  • ❌ We never use AI to train models on your personal family records without explicit consent.

✊8. Your Rights

We honor data rights for all users, regardless of jurisdiction.

  • Right to Access: Request a complete copy of all data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct any inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Right to Portability: Export your family tree in GEDCOM or PDF format at any time.
  • To exercise any of these rights, contact us at our contact page.

Questions About Our Privacy Practices?

We're committed to transparency. If you have any questions, please reach out — we respond within 2 business days.
