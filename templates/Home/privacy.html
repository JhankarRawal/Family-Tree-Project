Privacy & Security

Privacy Policy

We believe your family's history belongs to you — and only you. Here is exactly how we protect it.

📋

Last Updated: January 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the Family Tree Web Application collects, uses, and protects your information. By using our platform, you agree to these terms.

🔑

Data Ownership

You own your data. Every piece of information you add — names, dates, photos, and relationship records — belongs entirely to you and your family.

  • Full intellectual property rights over all content you create.
  • Export your complete family tree anytime in PDF or GEDCOM format.
  • Account and data deletion within 30 days.
  • No ownership claimed by the platform.
🌳

Private Family Trees

Privacy is the default — not an option. Every family tree is private and accessible only to invited people.

  • Private by default, access only by invitation.
  • Control collaborator permissions individually.
  • Revoke access instantly at any time.
  • Living persons' details obscured automatically.
🔐

Secure Authentication

  • Passwords hashed with PBKDF2 + SHA-256.
  • CSRF protection on all web forms.
  • HttpOnly & Secure cookies.
  • Rate-limited login attempts.
  • Password reset links expire in 24h.
  • TLS/HTTPS encryption for all connections.
👥

Access Control

  • Owner: Full control.
  • Editor: Add/edit members, no permissions management.
  • Viewer: Read-only access.
  • Internal staff cannot access without request.
  • All access events logged.
🛡️

Data Protection

  • Encrypted PostgreSQL databases.
  • Daily encrypted backups.
  • ISO 27001-certified data centers.
  • Regular security audits.
  • Breach notifications within 72 hours.
📊

Information Collected

  • Account data: Name, email, hashed password.
  • Family tree data: All genealogical records added.
  • Usage logs: Activity within your tree.
  • Technical logs: Server logs retained 90 days.
  • Cookies: Authentication only, no tracking.
⚖️

Responsible Data Usage

  • ✅ Display family tree & platform features.
  • ✅ Security notifications & password resets.
  • ✅ Aggregated anonymized usage stats.
  • ❌ No selling or sharing to third parties.
  • ❌ No AI training without consent.
✊

Your Rights

  • Right to Access, Rectification, Erasure, Portability.
  • Contact via contact page.
