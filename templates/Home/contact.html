Common Questions

Before You Write to Us

You might find an instant answer here.

How quickly do you respond?

We aim to respond to all messages within 24 hours on business days. For urgent security issues, we prioritize response within 4 hours.

Do you offer customer support in other languages?

Currently our primary support language is English. We are working to expand multilingual support and appreciate your patience.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. You'll receive a password reset email within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

Can I request a feature?

Absolutely. We love hearing from users about the features that would make the biggest difference. Use the contact form above with the subject "Feature Request".
