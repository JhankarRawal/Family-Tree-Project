All Features

Everything Your Family Needs

A comprehensive toolkit designed to make genealogy research intuitive, collaborative, and secure.

🌳
Family Tree Visualization

Render your family tree as an interactive, zoomable diagram powered by D3.js. Explore generational branches with smooth animations, collapsible nodes, and intuitive pan-and-zoom controls. Every relationship is rendered clearly, making complex family structures easy to understand at a glance.

👥
Person Management

Add, edit, and manage detailed profiles for every family member. Store names, birth dates, biographical notes, photos, and life events. Rich profile pages give each person their own complete record within your family's digital archive.

🔗
Relationship Management

Define precise family relationships — parent, child, spouse, sibling, and more. The system intelligently validates connections and prevents logical conflicts, ensuring your family tree always represents accurate genealogical data.

✉️
Family Invitation System

Invite relatives to collaborate on your shared family tree via email invitations. Manage roles and permissions to control who can view, edit, or administer your family records. Build your tree together, wherever your family lives.

🔍
Advanced Search

Locate any relative instantly using our PostgreSQL full-text search engine. Search by name, relationship type, birth year, or any biographical detail. Fuzzy matching and ranking ensure you always find the right person even with incomplete information.

📋
Activity Logs

Track every change made to your family tree with timestamped activity logs. See who added a member, edited a relationship, or updated a profile — giving your family full transparency and the ability to review or revert any modification.

📤
Export Options

Export your family history in multiple formats. Generate beautifully formatted PDF reports for printing and sharing, or export standard GEDCOM files compatible with any genealogy software. Your data is always portable and yours to keep.

🔒
Privacy Controls

Maintain complete control over your family's private information. Every tree is private by default. Granular access permissions let you decide exactly who can see, edit, or manage each part of your genealogy records. Security is built into the foundation.

📱
Responsive Access

Access your family tree from any device — desktop, tablet, or smartphone. The fully responsive interface ensures a seamless experience whether you're at your desk or browsing from the field during genealogy research.
